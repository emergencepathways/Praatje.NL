# Praatje.NL roadmap

## 0.1 — Reader, word list, Anki ✅
- Reader panel for dialogue, cutscenes, answer choices, letters and notes; every word clickable; +EN column.
- Word list ("Mijn woorden"), straight-to-Anki sync (shared Speurtocht.NL deck and note type), .txt export, clipboard.
- Dictionary batches 1–2 (939 word forms, 80.6% of the game's Dutch text), spelling rules, Wiktionary fallback.

## Dictionary batches (ongoing)
13,520 different words in the Dutch mod; 500 per batch, most frequent first.

| Batches | Coverage of all Dutch text |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | 80.6% |
| 3–4 | ~87% |
| 5–8 | ~93% |
| 9–16 | ~97% |
| 17–27 | 100% (rare words, many used once) |

## 0.2 — More text
- Quest log and quest descriptions, Help Wanted board, Special Orders board.
- TV channels (they use the dialogue box: check what's missing), item descriptions in tooltips, shop text.
- Speech bubbles above villagers' heads.

## 0.3 — Click inside the dialogue box
- Hold a key (default Left Shift): the dialogue box and letter stop advancing and their own words become clickable.
  Needs a copy of the game's `SpriteText` line breaking to know where each word is.

## 0.4 — Learning extras
- Difficulty colouring: words you saved before, words you haven't met yet.
- Per-word "seen" counter across the game; review list of words seen often but not saved.
- A Dutch/English switch for the mod's own interface.

## 1.0 — Public release
- Nexus Mods page, screenshots, licence for code and meanings, issue template for wrong meanings.
